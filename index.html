Spec — Calibration

No Calibration Required

Strain-gauge systems drift with temperature and mechanical wear, requiring regular re-zeroing. Balance Mat reads micro-movements through changes in light transmission instead of electrical resistance — designed to avoid the electrical drift that affects strain-gauge systems.
